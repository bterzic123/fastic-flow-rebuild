# Growth Audit — Fastic

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/fastic-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas plus a clickable prototype of Fastic's onboarding and PLUS screen, rebuilt in Fastic's own design: the #1D1D1D screens, the answer cards, the green Continue, the orange progress dashes, the bear and the meal-time wheels. Three phones, one for each promise in Fastic's Meta ads: the AI food scanner, fasting instead of exercise, and before-and-after.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Expected impact |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Put one date and their occasion in the PLUS headline | CR +15–20% |
| 2 | Build the plan around how they said they'd do it (fasting, tracking or both) | CR +10–20% |
| 3 | Let the loader read their answers back | CR +10–15% |
| 4 | Show your 4.7 stars where they choose a plan | CR +10–15% · ARPU +2–5% |
| 5 | Start on the plan you call Most popular, and say how it bills | CR +5–15% · ARPU +3–8% |

The X on PLUS closes straight into the app. There is no second paywall.

## Grounding

Screens, copy and prices come from the Fastic iOS app as recorded on 11 November 2026 (EU storefront: €2.34, €1.60 and €3.05 a week). The rating (4.7 from 131,129 ratings), the PLUS plans and the quoted reviews come from the German App Store listing. The ads come from the Meta Ad Library. Dates on the plan and PLUS are computed from each user's answers, at the pace of Fastic's own plan (13.1 kg over 26 weeks).

*Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Fastic.*
